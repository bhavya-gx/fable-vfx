# Revisions

Talk in seconds of the finished video, say what is wrong and what you want. The model changes the config and re-renders only what it needs to.

```text
Revise configs/[name].json and re-render.

- 18.4: the star is too high, bring it down about 40 px.
- 9.4: the ocean wipe starts from the wrong hand, use my right hand.
- 22.0: swap the B for the word "GO", in mint.
- The title fades too early, keep it until 3.2.
- Make the whole thing 1.15x.

Show me stills of each changed moment before the full render.
```

Things you can ask for without any code changes: a different title or font, the moments, which hand, how a prop enters (fly in or pop up), its size, colour (gold, pink, sky, mint, or your own), the vanish time, speed, how loud the effects are, no grade, no ocean, no title, more props.

Things that need code: a new kind of prop, a different background than the ocean, portrait output. Ask, and the model should tell you what it would add before adding it.
